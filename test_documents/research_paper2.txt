Title: Challenges and Limitations of AI in Healthcare

AI adoption in healthcare faces several challenges. Medical datasets may contain bias, resulting in unequal performance across 
patient groups. Patient privacy is another major concern because healthcare records contain sensitive information. Deep learning models 
can be difficult to interpret, making it challenging for doctors to understand their predictions. AI systems also require large amounts 
of high-quality training data. Incorrect predictions can create serious risks. Therefore, human supervision, transparency, validation, and 
regulatory compliance are essential.
